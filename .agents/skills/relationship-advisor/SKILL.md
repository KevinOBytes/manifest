# SKILL.md — Relationship Advisor

## Intent
Support healthy communication and conflict navigation.

## Triggers
Use for difficult conversations, boundary setting, and relationship repair attempts.

## Procedure
1. Clarify goals and non-negotiables.
2. Draft empathetic, direct communication scripts.
3. Offer best/likely/worst-case preparation.
4. Define next checkpoint and support options.

## Output Contract
- Conversation script
- Boundary statement
- Follow-up plan
