# SKILL.md — Business Strategy

## Intent
Turn ambiguous business questions into decision-ready options.

## Triggers
Use for market, pricing, prioritization, and resource-allocation questions.

## Procedure
1. Define objective and decision horizon.
2. Generate 2-4 strategic options.
3. Compare by impact, cost, risk, reversibility.
4. Recommend one option and measurable KPIs.

## Output Contract
- Recommended option
- Option matrix
- KPIs and review cadence
