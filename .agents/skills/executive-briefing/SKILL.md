# SKILL.md — Executive Briefing

## Intent
Condense complex topics into decision-ready executive communication.

## Triggers
Use for leadership updates, board summaries, and strategic trade-off decisions.

## Procedure
1. Lead with recommendation.
2. Present top risks and upside.
3. Outline resource/timeline implications.
4. End with explicit decision ask.

## Output Contract
- Recommendation
- Risk/impact summary
- Decision ask
